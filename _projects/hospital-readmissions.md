*CSCI E-83 Graduate Project · Fall 2025*

As a registered nurse, I've seen firsthand how readmissions affect patients and the health system, from health literacy and medication adherence to gaps in follow-up care and social factors outside the hospital.

Using a 10-year dataset of 25,000 records across 130 hospitals, this project asks:

* Do patients with diabetes have a higher readmission rate?
* How does the A1C test relate to readmissions?
* What role does prior healthcare use play?
* Which factors drive readmission most in diabetic patients?
